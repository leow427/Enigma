Ferry Timetable Changes

The winter ferry timetable starts on the first Monday of November, when the early crossing moves forty minutes later and the last evening boat leaves the island at half past eight instead of ten.

Passengers with season tickets do not need to renew them, and the ticket office on the pier will keep its summer opening hours until the end of the month so that anyone with questions can ask in person.

The operator says the changes follow a survey of regular travellers, most of whom asked for a later first boat on school days rather than a late return on weekday evenings.
