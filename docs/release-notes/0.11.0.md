# Enigma 0.11.0

Bigger local models that stay fast, chosen in one place.

- **Recommendations fit your Mac's real speed.** Enigma measures your Mac's memory bandwidth with a quick GPU test and recommends the smartest model that still replies quickly. On a 24 GB M5 Pro that is now Gemma 4 12B instead of Qwen3.5 4B.
- **Three clear choices.** Every picker shows the same **recommended**, **faster** and **smarter, slower replies** options.
- **A refreshed catalogue.** New: Qwen3.5 2B, Qwen3.6 35B-A3B and Qwen3.8 27B, plus more sizes of Qwen3.5 9B, Gemma 4 12B and Gemma 4 26B-A4B, for Macs from 8 GB to 128 GB. SmolVLM2, Gemma 4 E4B, Ministral 3 and MiniCPM-o leave the catalogue; installed copies keep working under **On this Mac**.
- **Models no longer in the catalogue get no update.** They, including Gemma 4 12B QAT, stay on their old llama.cpp runtime, which misreads screenshots on Gemma models. If you use Gemma 4 12B QAT for screenshots, install the new Gemma 4 12B from Settings → Local models.
- **One Local models page.** Settings → Discover and Hugging Face file downloads are gone. Settings → Local models shows the choices for this Mac, your installed models and every catalogue model.
- **Check this Mac** replaces **Detect Mac capabilities** and **Refresh recommendations**, and measures the bandwidth again.
- **A newer llama.cpp (b11734)** reads full-screen screenshots on Gemma 4 correctly. Installed models show **Update** once, which downloads the package again; until then the installed copy keeps working.
